Regulated Diagnostic Biostatistics: a ten-day crash course

Author

Ignacio

Ten days, about two hours each. Goal: answer interview questions on regulated diagnostic biostatistics precisely, name the governing document for each design, and run each standard analysis in R on simulated data.

Role context. Entry-level Biostatistician / Data Scientist, Biostatistics & Personalized Healthcare (Roche Diagnostics). Flagship public programme: Alzheimer’s blood biomarkers (Elecsys pTau217 etc.).

Reading conventions

  • [S] = stated in a primary public source (cited in claims_ledger.csv).
  • [Y] = my synthesis of standard theory; not a quotation.
  • “not verified: paywalled” = could not be checked; do not state it in an interview as fact.
  • CLSI texts are paywalled. Nothing here quotes them.
  • No Roche assay performance numbers are invented. Numbers for the pTau217 test come from the FDA decision summary, with citation.

Corrections to the starting brief (verified)

  1. EP05 is now Ed4 (Dec 2025); EP25, EP26, EP35 and EP19 also have newer editions than the brief lists.
  2. IVDR Arts 47–48 are classification and conformity assessment; the GSPR are in Annex I. CDx are class C (Rule 3(f)); the medicines-authority consultation is in Art 48(7)/(8) for class C (and 48(3)/(4) for class D).
  3. FDA’s 2020 CDx guidance is titled “…for a Specific Group of Oncology Therapeutic Products” (final); “Group or Class” was the draft. The 2016 codevelopment guidance is still a draft.
  4. pTau217: FDA decision 2026-08-19 (K261686); Roche announced on 2026-08-24. The two cited papers used a prototype assay, not the cleared one.
