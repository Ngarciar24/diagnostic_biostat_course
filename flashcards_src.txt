1|IVDR Article 47 covers what?|Classification of devices (classes A-D, per Annex VIII). Not the safety/performance requirements.
1|IVDR Article 48 covers what?|Conformity assessment procedures. CDx medicines-authority consultation: 48(3)/(4) class D, 48(7)/(8) class C.
1|Where are the IVDR general safety and performance requirements (GSPR)?|Annex I (Art 5 requires compliance). Performance characteristics: Annex I Chapter II, Section 9.
1|IVDR Annex I 9.1(a) lists which analytical parameters?|Analytical sensitivity and specificity, trueness (bias), precision, limits of detection and quantitation, measuring range, linearity, cut-off.
1|IVDR Annex I 9.1(b) lists which clinical parameters?|Diagnostic sensitivity, diagnostic specificity, PPV, NPV, likelihood ratio, expected values in normal and affected populations.
1|IVDR Art 2(7) defines what?|Companion diagnostic: a device essential for the safe and effective use of a corresponding medicinal product.
1|IVDR Art 2(38) defines what?|Scientific validity of an analyte: the association of an analyte with a clinical condition or physiological state.
1|IVDR Art 2(40) vs 2(41)?|2(40) analytical performance (correctly detect/measure an analyte); 2(41) clinical performance (results correlated with a clinical condition).
1|IVDR Art 2(44)?|Performance evaluation: assessment and analysis of data to establish or verify scientific validity, analytical and, where applicable, clinical performance.
1|IVDR Art 56 covers?|Performance evaluation and clinical evidence: three pillars (scientific validity, analytical, clinical performance); class C/D report updated at least annually (56(6)).
1|IVDR Art 57 vs Art 58?|57 general requirements for performance studies; 58 additional requirements for interventional/invasive studies, 58(2) includes companion diagnostic studies.
1|Which IVDR rule makes CDx class C?|Annex VIII Rule 3(f). Class C, not D.
1|Who is consulted for a CDx under IVDR?|A competent authority designated under Directive 2001/83/EC or the EMA, as applicable (Art 48(7)/(8), Annex IX 5.2).
1|IVDR classes and example rules?|A Rule 5 (general lab products); B Rules 6-7 (default; controls); C Rule 3 group and Rule 4 self-tests; D Rule 1 (e.g. blood-donation agents).
1|What does Annex XIII Part A contain?|Performance evaluation plan (PEP), performance evaluation report (PER), clinical performance study plan (2.3.2), study report.
1|What does Annex XIII Part B contain?|Post-market performance follow-up (PMPF).
1|IVDR transition (Reg 2024/1860): legacy certificate end date?|31 December 2027 (valid Directive certificates). First-time NB devices: class D 2027, C 2028, B/sterile A 2029, with conditions.
1|MDCG 2022-2 is?|Guidance on general principles of clinical evidence for IVDs (January 2022). Not legally binding.
1|ISO 20916:2019 is?|IVD medical devices: clinical performance studies using specimens from human subjects, good study practice. Analytical studies out of scope.
1|510(k) / De Novo / PMA and their verbs?|510(k) substantial equivalence: cleared. De Novo no predicate: granted. PMA class III: approved. (Verb mapping = synthesis.)

2|EP05-A3 single-site design?|20 days x 2 runs x 2 replicates per sample (Ed4 design not verified: paywalled).
2|Within-lab SD from components?|sqrt(var_day + var_run + var_repeatability). Variances add, SDs do not.
2|CV% formula?|100 x SD / mean at the level (report at medical decision levels).
2|Why can an ANOVA variance component be negative?|(MS_between - MS_within)/k can fall below 0 by chance; report as 0 and say so.
2|How is a CI for a variance component obtained?|Chi-square with Satterthwaite degrees of freedom (VCA::VCAinference).
2|Satterthwaite df of within-lab SD in a 20x2x2 study?|About 70, not 79; the day term has only 19 df.
2|EP15 in one line?|Short lab verification (public: within 5 working days; bias >= 25 measurements over >= 5 days) of manufacturer claims.
2|Upper verification limit for a claimed SD?|claim x sqrt(chi2_0.95,nu / nu) (one-sided chi-square test; synthesis).
2|Balanced nested design: ANOVA vs REML?|Identical estimates unless a component is truncated at 0.
2|Why many days in a precision study?|Day variance is only estimable from independent days; df of day drives CI width.
2|Bayesian vs EP05 variance model?|Same structure; priors shrink SDs, intervals are credible not confidence; use sparingly for claims.
2|Repeatability vs within-lab vs reproducibility?|Within-run; all single-site sources; adds between-site/instrument/lot.
3|Parametric LoB?|mean_blank + 1.645 x SD_blank.
3|Parametric LoD?|LoB + 1.645 x SD_low (EP17 small-sample correction not verified: paywalled).
3|Nonparametric LoB?|95th percentile of ranked blank results.
3|Nonparametric LoD?|Lowest level at which >= 95% of results exceed the LoB.
3|LoQ definition?|Lowest concentration meeting a predefined error goal (CV or total error).
3|Total error formula (synthesis)?|abs(bias) + 1.96 x SD, compared with allowable total error.
3|Probit LoD95?|Fit probit(p) = b0 + b1 log10(conc); LoD95 = 10^((1.645 - b0)/b1).
3|Which error rates define LoB and LoD?|alpha 5% (false positive on blank) and beta 5% (false negative at LoD).
3|When does LoD matter clinically?|When the decision level is near zero; otherwise precision and bias at the cut-off matter more.
3|Linearity evaluation (EP06 style, synthesis)?|Dilution series; linear vs polynomial fit; judge deviation against an allowable limit, not just the F-test.
3|Why can a significant linearity test still pass?|Statistical curvature can be smaller than the allowable deviation; decide on deviation limits.
4|Why not OLS for method comparison?|OLS assumes an error-free x; errors in both methods need Deming or Passing-Bablok.
4|Deming regression assumes?|Normal errors with a known error ratio; sensitive to outliers.
4|Passing-Bablok assumes?|Linear relation, positively correlated results; robust to outliers (shifted median of pairwise slopes).
4|Bias at decision level Xc?|bias = a + (b - 1) Xc; CI by jackknife (Deming) or bootstrap (PaBa).
4|Bland-Altman with proportional bias?|Plot relative differences vs mean; regress difference on level; limits of agreement are not decision-level bias.
4|Interference conclusion rule?|The whole CI of the difference must lie inside the allowable error; non-significance is not equivalence.
4|Shelf life from stability regression (synthesis)?|Time where the one-sided 95% bound of the mean line meets the acceptance limit.
4|EP26 in one line?|User evaluation of a new reagent lot with patient samples against a medically acceptable difference.
4|EP25 Ed2 in one line?|Regression-based reagent stability (shelf life and in-use).
4|What does EP07 cover?|Screening, quantifying and confirming interference; chemical interferents only.
4|EP09c scope?|Method comparison and bias estimation with patient samples (qualitative = EP12; precision = EP05/15; total error = EP21).
5|Sensitivity formula?|TP/(TP+FN) = P(T+ given D+).
5|Specificity formula?|TN/(TN+FP) = P(T- given D-).
5|PPV from Se, Sp, prevalence?|Se x prev / (Se x prev + (1 - Sp)(1 - prev)).
5|LR+ and LR-?|Se/(1 - Sp) and (1 - Se)/Sp; independent of prevalence.
5|PPV of Se 0.90, Sp 0.85 at 1% prevalence?|0.009/0.1575 = 5.7%.
5|FDA 2007 on non-reference comparators?|Sensitivity and specificity are not appropriate terms; report positive and negative percent agreement.
5|PPA and NPA formulas?|PPA = positive agreement among comparator-positive; NPA = negative among comparator-negative; asymmetric.
5|Discrepant resolution problem?|Only discordant results are resolved; errors shared by both tests are never corrected, so the new test is flattered.
5|Verification bias effect?|Naive sensitivity too high, specificity too low when positives are verified more often.
5|Begg-Greenes correction needs?|Verification depends only on the test result (MAR); uses P(D given T) from verified and P(T) from all.
5|Wilson vs Clopper-Pearson?|Wilson: coverage near nominal, good near 0/1. Clopper-Pearson: exact, conservative.
5|Compare sensitivities of two tests on the same subjects?|McNemar on discordant pairs among the diseased; same for specificity among the non-diseased.
5|AUC meaning?|P(random case result > random control result) (Mann-Whitney).
5|DeLong method?|Nonparametric variance/covariance of correlated AUCs from structural components (Biometrics 1988;44:837).
5|Partial AUC purpose?|Summarise only the clinically relevant range (e.g. specificity 0.9-1); McClish standardisation to 0.5-1.
5|EP24-A2 in one line?|ROC-based diagnostic accuracy concepts for continuous tests; not a recipe.
5|EP12 Ed3 in one line?|Binary-output examinations: imprecision near cut-off, sensitivity/specificity, stability, interference.
