## Discrepancies with the working list
- **EP05**: working list EP05-A3 is outdated; Ed4 (2025-12-11) is current. Course design numbers from EP05-A3 (20 x 2 x 2) must be labelled as Ed3; Ed4 design not verified: paywalled/unavailable (abstract states no numbers).
- **EP25**: "EP25-A" (2009) is superseded by EP25-Ed2 (2023).
- **EP26**: "EP26-A" (2013) is superseded by EP26-Ed2 (2022).
- **EP35**: Ed2 (2025) is current, not Ed1 (2019).
- **EP19**: Ed3 (2022) current; Ed2 was 2015.
- **EP15**: EP15-A3 confirmed; no Ed4 on the CLSI page. Note the 2021 IG1/IG2 implementation guides and an EP15 workbook correction notice (May 2024).
- **EP17**: EP17-A2 confirmed; no Ed3 on the CLSI page.
- **EP24-A2, EP28-A3c, EP06-Ed2, EP07-Ed3, EP09c-Ed3, EP12-Ed3**: confirmed. Several (EP17, EP24, EP28, EP15) are old reaffirmed documents; EP28-A3c in particular dates from 2010.
- EP37: edition number not verified.

## Design facts stated publicly
- EP05-A3 (Ed3): single-site protocol 20 days x 2 runs/day x 2 replicates per sample/lot. Source: CLSI press release https://clsi.org/about/press-releases/clsi-released-an-updated-standard-on-evaluation-of-precision-of-quantitative-measurement-procedures/ (Sept 2014). Ed4 design: not verified: paywalled/unavailable.
- EP15-A3: precision/bias protocol "designed to be completed within five working days"; bias section needs 25 or more measurements over 5 or more days (CLSI product page). Replicates per day (commonly cited as 5 x 5): not verified: paywalled/unavailable (the press release says EP15 was adapted to EP05 single-site designs but gives no numbers). FDA decision summaries / peer-reviewed papers not searched; not verified.
- EP06-Ed2: two designs (a high sample known to exceed the measuring interval plus a measurand-free sample; or high and low samples with known concentrations/ratio). Replicates/levels: not verified: paywalled/unavailable.
- EP17-A2, EP07, EP09c, EP12, EP28, EP24, EP26: abstracts give no days/runs/replicate or sample numbers. EP26: two-stage protocol (Stage 1 one-time setup of medically acceptable difference/risks; Stage 2 routine per-lot) with "a small number of patient samples" (no number). EP28 sample size (e.g. 120): not verified: paywalled/unavailable.
- EP25-Ed2: regression-based; data needed at and beyond claimed time; drops slope t-test and CI requirement in acceptance criteria.
